Why Stay With Us

Hostel Facilities at a Glance

Everything a student needs for a focused and comfortable academic life.

🛏️ Comfortable Rooms

Shared and single-occupancy rooms with proper ventilation, study tables, cupboards and 24-hour electricity backup.

🍽️ Hygienic Mess

Nutritious vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals prepared under strict hygiene standards, with a rotating weekly menu.

🛡️ Round-the-Clock Security

CCTV-monitored premises, a manned entry gate and a resident warden to ensure student safety at all times.

📶 Wi-Fi & Study Zones

Campus-wide internet connectivity and a quiet reading room for group study and exam preparation.

💳 Online Fee Payment

Book your room and pay hostel & mess fees online with instant digital receipts - no paperwork required.

🧺 Housekeeping

Regular cleaning of rooms, washrooms and common areas along with laundry facility on campus.
