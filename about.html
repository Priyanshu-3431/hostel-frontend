About Us

About GP BARH Hostel

GP BARH Hostel is the official residential facility of Government Polytechnic Barh, established to provide safe, disciplined and affordable accommodation to diploma students coming from different parts of Bihar and beyond. Our aim is to give every resident a comfortable environment that supports their academic growth.

🏠 Student Accommodation

Separate hostel blocks for boys and girls with shared and single-seater rooms, each equipped with a cot, mattress, study table, chair and cupboard. Rooms are allotted branch-wise wherever possible so that batchmates can study together.

🍛 Mess Facility

A dedicated mess hall serves breakfast, lunch, evening snacks and dinner on a fixed weekly rotation menu. Special care is taken to maintain hygiene, and feedback from the student mess committee is reviewed monthly.

📖 Hostel Rules

Residents are expected to maintain discipline, respect the entry/exit timings, keep their rooms clean, and avoid ragging or misconduct of any kind. Visitors are allowed only in the designated visiting area during permitted hours.

🔒 Safe & Comfortable Environment

CCTV surveillance at entry points, a resident warden on every floor, fire-safety equipment and a 24x7 security guard ensure that every student feels secure while living away from home.

What You Get as a Resident
